# Decoder objective and optimizer audit

Read-only audit of the completed reconstruction run. Existing step 1,000 and step 10,000 checkpoints were inspected on CPU. This note also incorporates the coordinating agent's separately recorded CPU forward and gradient diagnostics. No training updates, source edits, or GPU work were performed for this audit.

## What the saved checks actually demonstrated

The 32-step full-capacity Muon/AdamW synthetic run reduced total loss from 63.6302 to 11.6157, mainly by reducing teacher spectral error from 4.2353 to 0.7679. Waveform L1 only moved from 0.10044 to 0.09727, about a 3.2% improvement. The all-AdamW synthetic arm was similar: total 11.7636 and waveform 0.09727. These are setup checks, not a demonstrated Muon convergence advantage or successful waveform overfit. [Saved preflight](../convnext-implementation/h100-logged-preflight.json).

The first speech run's fixed development waveform error was already nearly flat around 0.0377 to 0.0382 from step 300 to step 1,000 while spectral error improved. That was a reason to inspect waveform/phase quality before equating falling spectral totals with teacher-quality recovery. It does not prove the final model's phase is wrong. [Saved speech validation](../convnext-implementation/speech-warmup-verification.json).

## What the completed-model diagnostics demonstrate

The coordinating agent evaluated 29 held-out utterances using 58 start/interior crops on CPU, without training updates. This deterministic diagnostic panel spans speech and expressive sources but is not a population quality estimate. The results are means across its scored crops. [Diagnostic data](completed-loss-diagnostics.json).

| Prediction | Composite loss | Teacher waveform L1 | Mean per-crop RMS ratio to teacher | Mean zero-lag cosine to teacher |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Exact teacher | 20.703 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| Silence | 81.363 | 0.02594 | 0 | Undefined |
| Student at 1,000 | 32.688 | 0.02672 | 0.236 | 0.0043 |
| Student at 10,000 | 24.847 | 0.02734 | 0.342 | 0.0113 |

The teacher itself retains a substantial composite loss because the original-reference term is nonzero. That calibrates the numerical total, but it does not explain away the student's waveform problem: its output is much quieter on average, waveform L1 is worse than silence, and zero-lag agreement is very low on this panel. These measurements do not establish listening quality by themselves or distinguish an alignment offset from phase error. They do show that the reduced spectral total has not established teacher waveform fidelity. The teacher score is a comparison baseline, not a proved minimum of this multi-target objective.

The weighted loss gradients were also differentiated with respect to the predicted audio for eight selected CREMA-D/Emogator crops. Reference spectral gradient norms were about 95 to 164, teacher spectral norms about 13 to 25, and waveform norms about 0.00285 to 0.00329. This is measured output-space gradient imbalance, beyond the earlier observation about numerical loss contributions. It is not a measurement of parameter-update contributions: the network Jacobian and optimizers can change those ratios. The small panel also does not establish a universal gradient ratio or a strongly conflicting gradient direction. Parameter-gradient and alignment checks are the next relevant evidence before attributing the failure to a specific objective component.

## Optimizer and model update checks

The hybrid is implemented as intended: native Muon updates the twenty hidden expand/project matrices, and AdamW updates the other eighty student parameter tensors. Teacher parameters are frozen and absent. Bootstrap import restores both optimizer states, student weights, RNG and global step; group identities are checked. [Routing](/Users/venky/Documents/Codex/2026-09-07/https-arxiv-org-html-2402-10533v2/work/fast-audiovae/experiments/convnext/audiovae_student/optimizers.py:44), [state restoration](/Users/venky/Documents/Codex/2026-09-07/https-arxiv-org-html-2402-10533v2/work/fast-audiovae/experiments/convnext/audiovae_student/source_training.py:89).

Both final optimizer groups retain base learning rate 0.0002 and weight decay 0.01. Muon retains momentum 0.95, Nesterov, five Newton–Schulz steps and match_rms_adamw. AdamW retains betas 0.9/0.999. The schedule has 100 warmup updates and no subsequent decay. Constant learning rate and the eightfold batch increase are unvalidated optimization choices, not identified code defects. The proposed matched speech comparison against AdamW remains absent.

The blocks are not frozen at the LayerScale initialization of 0.000001. Mean absolute learned scale per block grew from 0.0073–0.0108 at step 1,000 to 0.0137–0.0201 at step 10,000. Every block's depthwise, expansion and projection weights changed substantially. Expansion/projection changes have Frobenius norms 0.78–1.33 times their respective step-1,000 weight norms. Adapter, stem, head, PReLU and output weights also changed. This rules out the simple explanation that the residual branches never learned or the optimizer never stepped; it does not measure branch activation usefulness. [Checkpoint tensor audit](block-and-optimizer-checkpoint-audit.json).

The logged gradient norm is before clipping. A value above one is compatible with max_grad_norm=1 working correctly. Muon and AdamW subsequently transform gradients, so clipping does not bound the final weight-update norm. Finite checks reject missing/nonfinite gradients and nonfinite parameters, but cannot establish perceptual quality.

## Conditioning, context and target consistency

The pinned teacher receives mono 16 kHz audio, returns raw posterior means with 64 channels at 25 Hz, uses no stochastic noise block, and always decodes with 48 kHz conditioning. The student receives exactly those raw means and always produces 48 kHz. It therefore needs no additional variable sample-rate input for this task. [Teacher contract](/Users/venky/Documents/Codex/2026-09-07/https-arxiv-org-html-2402-10533v2/work/fast-audiovae/experiments/convnext/audiovae_student/teacher.py:121), [explicit conditioning](/Users/venky/Documents/Codex/2026-09-07/https-arxiv-org-html-2402-10533v2/work/fast-audiovae/experiments/convnext/audiovae_student/teacher.py:228).

Targets are generated from whole utterances before cropping. The student's 29 latent frames of left context cover its 116 internal-frame receptive field; context-only samples and invalid tails are removed before every loss FFT. Batching adds only right padding and averages by example count, preserving serial mean-loss semantics. [Cache generation](/Users/venky/Documents/Codex/2026-09-07/https-arxiv-org-html-2402-10533v2/work/fast-audiovae/experiments/convnext/audiovae_student/cache.py:176), [crop slices](/Users/venky/Documents/Codex/2026-09-07/https-arxiv-org-html-2402-10533v2/work/fast-audiovae/experiments/convnext/audiovae_student/cache.py:292), [batch reduction](/Users/venky/Documents/Codex/2026-09-07/https-arxiv-org-html-2402-10533v2/work/fast-audiovae/experiments/convnext/audiovae_student/batching.py:119).

A source-code support calculation finds the teacher decoder needs at most 20 previous latent frames, fewer than the student's 29. For each teacher residual stack, the three kernel-seven convolutions with dilations 1, 3 and 9 add 78 high-rate left samples. Reverse through each stride-r transpose convolution with earliest input floor(t/r)-1, plus the initial/final kernel-seven layers. Applying rates 8/6/5/2/2/2 gives a maximum 20-frame past dependency. This is an architectural calculation, not a new empirical parity test. Teacher zero padding versus student edge replication is a deliberate architectural difference at utterance starts, not a proven crop-alignment defect.

The genuine target-consistency issue is that original-reference reconstruction and exact frozen-teacher imitation are not identical objectives. Even a student emitting the exact teacher waveform can retain reference spectral error. Conversely, a student can sacrifice some teacher matching to improve the original-reference term. The teacher-as-student loss is a useful calibration baseline, not a proved lower bound on the combined objective.

## Interpretation and next decision

The final total is dominated numerically by the reference term. The bounded output-gradient diagnostic now also establishes a large gradient imbalance relative to waveform L1 on its selected examples. For log magnitude above the clamp, the derivative scales inversely with magnitude; waveform L1 differentiates raw amplitude. The gradient audit required by the plan should now extend to parameter-gradient norms and directions, and phase-sensitive alignment, without assuming output-space ratios transfer unchanged to optimizer updates.

The most grounded next step is to resolve the objective mismatch and calibrate these diagnostics before choosing more steps, another optimizer, or a new architecture. A phase-capable perceptual stage remains unimplemented. This audit does not justify claiming that simply adding a GAN, changing 45, decaying the learning rate, or switching away from Muon will recover teacher quality. Each would need a bounded, separately identified experiment after review.
