# DLL-APNet review

DLL-APNet is useful evidence for distillation into a convolutionally causal vocoder. It is not yet a verified implementation meeting our stronger streaming contract.

The paper changes the APNet2 input/output convolutions and the depthwise convolution inside each ConvNeXt V2 block to causal versions. It says only the depthwise operator needs conversion within the block. It does not specify a change to GRN's time aggregation, nor the iSTFT padding, alignment or overlap state. Its experiment uses 16 kHz VCTK speech, a 320-sample window, 80-sample hop and 1024-point FFT, with eight blocks per branch. It reports 6.30 GFLOPs for one second of audio, not measured CPU RTF or latency in milliseconds. Feature distillation increases UTMOS from 3.90 to 3.98 versus its undistilled causal baseline; log-amplitude RMSE changes from 4.23 to 4.29. Teacher and student share architecture, so intermediate features can be compared directly. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.13667v1)

The author's repository currently exposes a README and a `docs` demo tree with audio groups, figures and a web page. I did not locate runnable model/training/streaming code. Therefore I cannot verify that GRN was removed, made causal or handled consistently between full-sequence training and streaming. [Repository](https://github.com/redmist328/DLL-APNet), [demo tree](https://github.com/redmist328/DLL-APNet/tree/main/docs)

The distinction matters because the released APNet2 GRN reduces over the complete time axis. Making only its depthwise convolution causal would leave another future-dependent operation. Its centered iSTFT also needs a defined overlap delay or a changed synthesis alignment. These are unresolved implementation questions, not proof that the unpublished DLL implementation is wrong. A frame length of 20 ms and hop of 5 ms do not by themselves establish a 5 ms or 20 ms end-to-end delay. [APNet2 implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/redmist328/APNet2/main/models.py)

For our system, replacing mel conditioning with 64-channel AudioVAE2 latents and changing output to 48 kHz would be a new adaptation. Direct layerwise L1 distillation also does not transfer mechanically: our AudioVAE2 teacher and ConvNeXt student have different rates, dimensions and hidden coordinate systems. Appropriate aligned targets or training-only projections need justification. The existing causal body could be retained with a new spectral head, but that would no longer reproduce the paper's two-branch architecture or its measured result.

Recommendation: cite DLL-APNet for the benefit of intermediate-feature distillation under causal constraints. Do not adopt its architecture on the assumption that its name proves strict zero-lookahead streaming or that its GFLOPs establish CPU speed. Obtain or independently verify GRN and iSTFT behavior first; keep this separate from testing already-transferable waveform-derived spectral supervision.
