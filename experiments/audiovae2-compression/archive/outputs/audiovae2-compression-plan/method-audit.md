# Method audit: thinning the trained AudioVAE2 decoder

**Recommend compressing a complete three-unit residual stack into a two-unit student stack, jointly retraining both retained units to match the original stack's output on the same input.** Retain every upsampling stage and the stack's channel count, rate, alignment and causal API. The supervision is at the whole group's boundary, not at individual internal layers. A one-unit student is a later, more aggressive option. This review used source code, saved arithmetic profiles and primary literature. No model execution, training or benchmark was performed.

## What “the same black box” can mean

The decoder has six rate-changing stages. Each contains Snake, causal transposed convolution, then three residual units with dilations 1, 3 and 9. A residual unit computes `x + PW(Snake(DW7(Snake(x))))`. The terminal Snake, neighboring-sample convolution and tanh remain intact. [Pinned source: residual unit](/Users/venky/Documents/Codex/2026-09-07/https-arxiv-org-html-2402-10533v2/work/convnext-natural-context-repair/upstream-source-audit/audio_vae_v2.py:75), [stage](/Users/venky/Documents/Codex/2026-09-07/https-arxiv-org-html-2402-10533v2/work/convnext-natural-context-repair/upstream-source-audit/audio_vae_v2.py:176), [output head](/Users/venky/Documents/Codex/2026-09-07/https-arxiv-org-html-2402-10533v2/work/convnext-natural-context-repair/upstream-source-audit/audio_vae_v2.py:310).

The intended construction is `T_group(x) = R3(R2(R1(x)))` and `S_group(x) = Q2(Q1(x))`, with **all parameters of Q1 and Q2 allowed to adapt together**. Inheriting two units provides an initialization; their internal outputs are free to change. There is no loss forcing Q1 to copy R1 or Q2 to copy R2. The target is the final output of the complete three-unit teacher stack. This is the concrete AudioVAE2 form of replacing a ten-layer black box with a six-layer black box that has the same external contract.

There are three different promises:

1. **Same interface:** identical external channels, output samples, temporal origin, valid region, causal access and reset/flush contract. Residual-unit deletion can satisfy this without adapters.
2. **Same function:** identical output for every admissible input history. Removing a trained nonlinear branch generally cannot satisfy this. It would require a proven identity, redundancy or an exact alternative factorization.
3. **Recovered quality:** sufficiently close behavior on representative unseen inputs, including silence and peaks. This is the experimental objective, not an algebraic guarantee.

Even exact real-number affine rewrites can change floating-point rounding. A replacement's streaming state need not have the same internal size, but must preserve the public stream behavior. A smaller receptive field can remain causal and correctly aligned while losing access to history the teacher uses.

**Net2Net is not an exact deletion theorem.** Its standard constructions expand width or depth under specific conditions. Net2DeeperNet requires an activation identity that ordinary Snake does not have. Inserting a zero residual branch can preserve a function; deleting a learned nonzero residual branch cannot use that argument. Compatible adjacent affine operators can sometimes be composed, but this decoder's important matrices are separated by nonlinearities, and temporal convolution composition also requires matching boundary and bias semantics. [Net2Net, Sections 2.3–2.4](https://arxiv.org/html/1511.05641).

## Candidate order and realistic leverage

The saved arithmetic count is 8.991 GMAC per output second: upsampling accounts for 56.12%, residual pointwise matrices 42.09%, and counted depthwise kernels 1.63%. These exclude activation and memory costs and are not current CPU timings. [Saved shapes, formulas and provenance](/Users/venky/Documents/Codex/2026-09-07/https-arxiv-org-html-2402-10533v2/outputs/intel-iteration2/data/architecture-costs.json).

| Candidate | Stage boundary preserved? | Why consider it / principal limit |
|---|---|---|
| Distill a three-unit residual stack into two units | Yes, without a projection | Both retained units learn the complete teacher stack's mapping. Saves one unit's matrices, two Snakes and state; reduces nonlinear capacity and receptive field. |
| Distill that stack into one unit | Yes, without a projection | Larger reduction with less capacity to reproduce the same mapping. Consider only after assessing the two-unit student. |
| Factor an existing pointwise matrix into two linear matrices | Yes | Keeps every Snake and temporal filter. Rank reduction is approximate and adds a matrix call/intermediate buffer. |
| Reduce internal channel groups and restore boundary width | Only with correctly designed internal projections or coupled boundary handling | Greater arithmetic leverage, but more structural dependencies and implementation changes. |
| Remove an upsampling stage | No, by simple deletion | Changes output rate and sample count. A replacement with the same stride product is a new synthesis module, not layer pruning. |

Compressing one three-unit stack to two units in the largest 6 kHz, 256-channel stage removes **4.49% of counted decoder MACs**. Doing so in each of the 1.2, 6 and 12 kHz stages removes **10.35%**; doing so in every stage removes **14.57%**. These are exact deductions from the saved count, not measured RTF gains. Activation savings may help, while dispatch and memory costs may offset gains. A single compressed stack is a recovery diagnostic, not yet a candidate meeting a 10% runtime goal.

Do not automatically remove dilation 9 when choosing the inherited pair: all three units at a stage have equal counted MACs, but different temporal roles. Its dilation-9 filter contributes a nominal 270 ms of history at 200 Hz, versus 9 ms at 6 kHz. Short calibration crops can miss that consequence. Training-only removal impact can inform initialization, but the real decision is whether the jointly retrained smaller stack recovers the whole teacher mapping. An unfitted deletion's failure does not establish that the compressed stack cannot learn it. Small branch RMS alone is also insufficient because small contributions may cancel silence offsets.

If residual deletion is too destructive, **pointwise low rank is the cleaner next alternative under fixed stage interfaces**. For a square `C×C` matrix, factors `C×r` and `r×C` use `2Cr` MACs per position; they save arithmetic only below `r=C/2`. At `r=C/4`, that particular matrix uses half its previous MACs. Preserve bias and add no activation between factors. A full-rank decomposition can reproduce the matrix algebraically but usually provides no benefit. Truncated SVD is an initialization, not proof that perceptually important directions were retained. Low-rank CNN work supports approximation followed by recovery, and nonlinear/asymmetric reconstruction work specifically addresses accumulated errors. Its image-model speedups do not predict audio results. [Low-rank expansions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.3866), [nonlinear and asymmetric reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.06798).

## How to distill a replacement without misleading local success

1. **Authenticate an unchanged copy first.** Preserve the frozen encoder, raw 64-channel latents, teacher weights, initialization behavior and full-source boundaries. Establish copied-teacher parity before compression. This known starting function is the main advantage over a randomly initialized architecture.
2. **Define one complete replacement group.** Initially make the post-upsampling three-unit residual stack the group, keeping its surrounding upsampler and other stages unchanged. Construct a two-unit student from inherited units, then jointly fit every retained parameter inside that student stack. A zeroed dense branch still executes unless the exporter removes it. Do not combine this with width changes, activation changes or quantization in the first comparison.
3. **Match only the whole group output.** Evaluate the original teacher stack and smaller student stack on the same boundary feature sequence and history. Compare their final outputs directly, with no individual hidden-layer targets or channel projections. Retain final teacher-waveform and spectral supervision because small group-output error need not imply small audible error. Direct boundary matching leaves the student free to distribute the teacher's work differently internally.
4. **Distinguish prefix inputs as groups are replaced.** Let `xT` be the original teacher prefix's output and `xS` the current student prefix's output for the same latent sequence. Local initialization can fit `S_group(xT)` to `T_group(xT)`. When the upstream prefix changes, also train and check `S_group(xS)` against `T_group(xS)`, with the frozen teacher group's output used as a stop-gradient target. Do not call `S_group(xS)` versus `T_group(xT)` a same-input black-box test. The original teacher's final waveform on the original latent remains the end-to-end target, so locally consistent approximations are not allowed to drift together. Freezing suffix parameters must not detach its input gradients. Causal comparison requires the same complete input history or correctly rebuilt state, not merely equal current chunks. [Asymmetric reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.06798).
5. **Avoid the zero-score trap.** At an exact teacher copy, pure distillation error and its usual first-order gradients are zero, so that Taylor score cannot rank deletions. Evaluate actual removal impact on fitting data or use an explicitly justified nonzero criterion. A short fixed removal schedule could ease recovery, but adds a training variable; start with the simplest fixed candidate.
6. **Keep loss and data controls explicit.** Use the same fitting sources and exposure across candidates, calibrated waveform/spectral/feature weights, and actual optimizer-update diagnostics. Do not import the isolated repair runners' separately normalized quiet/active MSE. That imbalance was not the original AudioVAE2 recipe. A globally averaged stage loss must not be presented as proof of per-language, quiet or peak preservation.

Progressive module replacement has a concrete precedent in **BERT-of-Theseus**, with an author PyTorch implementation. It supports learning smaller substitutes inside a larger trained chain, but its stochastic schedule and NLP results are not evidence that this audio decoder needs that machinery. **LayerDrop** has an official fairseq implementation, but its pruning robustness is learned through structured-drop training. The existing teacher was not shown to have that preparation; its no-finetuning result cannot justify arbitrary deletion here. [BERT-of-Theseus paper](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.633/), [author implementation](https://github.com/JetRunner/BERT-of-Theseus), [LayerDrop paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.11556), [official implementation](https://github.com/facebookresearch/fairseq/blob/main/examples/layerdrop/README.md).

## Implementation and qualification gates

Weight normalization needs explicit handling: form the effective trained weight before slicing or decomposing it, then reconstruct the new parameterization. Slicing `weight_g` and `weight_v` independently can change normalization of retained weights. Width pruning must also couple depthwise groups, Snake alpha, pointwise axes, transposed-convolution axes, skips and streaming caches. **DepGraph/Torch-Pruning** is useful for dependency bookkeeping, not automatic certification of these custom causal wrappers. [DepGraph](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Fang_DepGraph_Towards_Any_Structural_Pruning_CVPR_2023_paper.html), [author implementation](https://github.com/VainF/Torch-Pruning).

The existing fused stage implementation expects three residual units. A two-unit exported candidate must execute two units, preserve cache correctness and use comparable optimized CPU execution. Comparing an optimized teacher against an unfused Python candidate would answer a different question.

Use fitting data for deletion selection, a development panel for limited decisions and a source-disjoint final panel for qualification. Check waveform and spectral errors, absolute quiet residual, transitions, peaks, expressive sounds and language groups, plus startup, reset, partial tails and multiple streaming chunk sizes. Include genuine encoded quiet audio; an all-zero latent fixture is a separate diagnostic. Retained tanh enforces bounded output but does not ensure correct peaks or silence. Stage reconstruction is a localization tool, not a substitute for final audio and streaming checks.

The practical order is **one complete stack distilled from three units to two, followed by a combined material-saving candidate only if that recovery is convincing**. An untrained deletion is optional diagnostic evidence, not the proposed endpoint or the qualification test. A one-unit stack can then test more aggressive compression without changing the group interface. Preserve the teacher and every accepted intermediate version. No paper establishes that this will retain all quality, need fewer data, match Supertonic's RTF, or achieve 4× acceleration. Those remain measurements to earn.
