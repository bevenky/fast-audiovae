Two verified precedents are relevant beyond StreamCodec2. Neither establishes that a channel-pruned AudioVAE2 decoder can retain all quality with fixed 64-channel latents.

| Method | Verified training transfer | What transfers to this project | Important limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| APCodec-S | MSE between corresponding convolution, feed-forward, modified ConvNeXt-v2 and quantizer outputs. Added to the original spectral, quantization and GAN losses; GAN training includes discriminator feature matching. KD weight is 1. | Intermediate generator hints are an established audio-codec technique, distinct from discriminator feature matching. Hints need no deployed operations. | Student encoder and quantizer are trained too. Corresponding widths are preserved; this does not establish unchanged teacher latent coordinates after channel pruning. |
| DLL-APNet | L1 teacher/student hints after the input convolution and each of eight ConvNeXt-v2 blocks; added to amplitude, phase, reconstructed-STFT and waveform objectives with adversarial training. Same mel input for teacher and student. | Decoder conditioning can remain fixed while intermediate hints guide a cheaper temporal implementation. Hints are training-only. | Causal conversion of an otherwise corresponding architecture, not a narrowed AudioVAE2 decoder. Evaluated on 16 kHz English VCTK, not our 48 kHz multilingual and expressive panel. |

APCodec-S uses a pretrained noncausal APCodec teacher. The student substitutes feed-forward layers for noncausal convolutions and causal upsampling for noncausal upsampling. Its paper reports a quality benefit from distillation, particularly phase behavior; it does not claim that hints eliminate the full quality gap or produce bitwise latent compatibility. Source: [APCodec §II-C, §III-B and §III-E](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.10533v2#S2.SS3).

DLL-APNet keeps causal depthwise convolutions instead of deleting their history. Its distillation objective is a sum of L1 discrepancies at the input-convolution output and all corresponding block outputs. Its study reports benefits from supervising more blocks, but also a decline when the KD coefficient exceeds the tested optimum. This argues against making intermediate targets dominate the final synthesis objectives. The paper reports FLOPs rather than establishing our required CPU RTF. Source: [DLL-APNet §3.3 and §5.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.13667v1#S3.SS3).

For this project, a direct adaptation would retain the frozen encoder and original latent coordinates, use actual compressed upstream activations, and add a small set of teacher boundary hints around the measured omission-sensitive upsamplers. Keep the existing full 128-channel group-output and waveform objectives. At narrowed internal boundaries, a training-only linear readout could predict the full teacher representation, or an explicitly defined selected-coordinate hint could supervise the inherited subspace. Either is our proposed adaptation, not a result proven by these papers. Selected-coordinate agreement must not be described as full feature reconstruction. A readout should be removed before inference; it must not be inserted into the deployed forward path.

The current diagnostic makes a first-mixer-only hint insufficient as a standalone fix: even its exact oracle leaves the subsequent omissions. It supports testing a small set of distributed targets around those later mixers rather than adding every possible loss. The balancing decision must still be judged by actual parameter-update directions and the held-out waveform, silence, peak and expressive metrics. These precedents do not justify a near-perfect waveform-correlation prerequisite before other useful training losses, or guarantee that additional hints will resolve capacity limits.

I did not verify an independent MDCTCodec-S distillation recipe. The [original MDCTCodec paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00464v1) does not describe a knowledge-distillation method; it should not be cited as a second independent KD result without a specific later source. LightCodec's full primary manuscript was not accessible in this bounded pass, so I have not assigned it an unverified distillation mechanism. No model execution, training, remote work or source changes were performed for this literature audit.
