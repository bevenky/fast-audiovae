# Author-referenced mel loss: exact implementation contract

The original AudioVAE collaborator [reply](https://github.com/OpenBMB/VoxCPM/issues/145#issuecomment-3767009845) explicitly selects the DAC seven-scale loss settings. This audit pins the referenced implementation and its current dependency definitions; it cannot recover the unpublished historical AudioVAE dependency lock. It is not a claim that the complete AudioVAE2 recipe was released.

At the pilot's 48 kHz rate, use windows/FFT sizes 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048 and corresponding mel counts 5, 10, 20, 40, 80, 160, 320. Hops are one quarter of the window. The class is named `MelSpectrogramLoss`, not `MultiScaleMelSpectrogramLoss`. [Pinned DAC configuration](https://github.com/descriptinc/descript-audio-codec/blob/c7cfc5d2647e26471dc394f95846a0830e7bec34/conf/base.yml#L61)

The actual audiotools default is ordinary periodic Hann, obtained through SciPy `get_window` and converted to FP32. Several audiotools docstrings incorrectly describe square-root Hann. `match_stride=False` gives no extra prepadding or dropped frames. `torch.stft` then centers with reflected boundary padding, without normalization, using a one-sided complex transform. Consequently a scored slice of length L contributes `L // hop + 1` frames. Existing center=False frame-count formulas cannot be reused. [Pinned audiotools implementation](https://github.com/descriptinc/audiotools/blob/348ebf2034ce24e2a91a553e3171cb00c0c71678/audiotools/core/audio_signal.py#L1064), [SciPy periodic default](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.signal.windows.get_window.html)

The mel bank uses librosa's Slaney frequency convention (`htk=False`) and Slaney area normalization, with FP32 weights, fmin=0, and fmax=Nyquist (24 kHz here). Apply the bank to complex STFT magnitude, then clamp at 1e-5, power one, and log10. Compute mean absolute differences within each scale and sum all seven scales. Raw-magnitude weight is zero; log weight is one. There is no division by seven and no factor of 10 or 20. [Pinned loss](https://github.com/descriptinc/descript-audio-codec/blob/c7cfc5d2647e26471dc394f95846a0830e7bec34/dac/nn/loss.py#L310), [Pinned librosa defaults](https://github.com/librosa/librosa/blob/f808bac0812469049fd8167c87f45813b7d57b32/librosa/filters.py#L87)

For this controlled pilot, compute each continuous valid source slice independently and pool by its real per-scale element counts across accumulated sources. Do not concatenate recordings or score zero-padded tails. This is the declared variable-length extension of the equal-length upstream batch reduction. Keeping raw teacher waveform and complete-group feature supervision is a compression-pilot choice. Its external coefficient need not equal the original generator's mel weight 15 because that generator used different companion losses. This A/B does not test GAN, KL, the unverified V2 low-band term, or the full pretraining schedule.

`source-manifest.json` records immutable URLs and file hashes. `verified-semantics.json` records settings and exact local source line ranges. No model, loss, or benchmark was executed in this audit.
